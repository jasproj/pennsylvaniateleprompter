Conference Teleprompter Options

Presidential Style Teleprompters

The iconic setup you see at political speeches and major keynotes. Two glass panels flanking the speaker reflect text from monitors below while remaining transparent to the audience. Perfect for:

  • Keynote addresses at large conferences
  • Political events and campaign rallies
  • Association annual meetings
  • Any event where the speaker needs to address a live audience while maintaining eye contact

Confidence Monitors

Floor-level displays that allow speakers to read their script while appearing to look directly at the audience. Ideal for:

  • Panel discussions and roundtables
  • Multi-speaker sessions
  • Workshops and training events
  • Presentations where glass teleprompters would be distracting

Camera-Mounted Teleprompters

For hybrid events with virtual components, our camera-mounted systems allow speakers to read while maintaining direct eye contact with the lens. Essential for:

  • Livestreamed conferences
  • Hybrid events with remote attendees
  • Recorded presentations for post-event distribution
  • Press conferences and media briefings
