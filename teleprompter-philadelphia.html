Frequently Asked Questions

How much does teleprompter rental cost in Philadelphia?

The day rate is the same across Pennsylvania — $600–$800 half day, $1,200–$1,500 full day, $1,500–$2,000 with a full kit. What changes in Philadelphia is travel: a presidential show adds a travel day from Pittsburgh; a camera-mount show can come from New York without one.

Do you provide teleprompter operators in Philadelphia?

Yes. Presidential and confidence-monitor shows are run by our Pittsburgh operator; camera-mount, jib, and Steadicam shoots can be staffed from our New York bench. We tell you which crew is on your date before you accept.

What types of events use teleprompter services in Philadelphia?

Convention center keynotes, university commencements and ceremonies, hospital foundation galas, corporate town halls, political addresses, and studio video shoots. Presidential glass for the stage; camera-mount or confidence monitors for everything else.

Why does a Philadelphia quote sometimes show a travel day?

Our presidential rig and its operator are based in Pittsburgh, about 300 miles west. A Philadelphia presidential show is a two-day commitment for that crew, so the quote carries one travel day plus IRS mileage. If your show is camera-mount, we can usually staff it from New York and skip the travel day.
